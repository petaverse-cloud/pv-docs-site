# Smart Location Tracking

WAGGURACY offers advanced **smart location tracking** designed to ensure the **safety** of your beloved pets. You can closely monitor your pet's **movements** at all times, knowing exactly where they are — at home, in the yard, or out on a walk.

![MAP](/img/map/Map.jpg)

## How to Use:

1. Log in to the **WAGGURACY** App:

    Open the **WAGGURACY** app on your Android or iOS device.

    **log in** to your account.
2. Access Instant Sync Location Tracking:

    Tap **Home**.

## Key Features:

In the WAGGURACY app, on the **Home** screen, as long as your pet is wearing PHAST 1 and it is working properly, you can view your pet's **current location** in instant sync on the map.

---

### Instant Sync Location Tracking

WAGGURACY combines multiple positioning technologies for fast, accurate indoor and outdoor location tracking — helping you stay connected to your pet wherever they go.

**Benefits:**

+ **Smart Wi-Fi Fence** on surrounding Wi-Fi fingerprint.
+ **Multi-system GNSS support** (GPS / BeiDou / GLONASS / other satellite modes).
+ **Fast, accurate indoor & outdoor pet locating.**
